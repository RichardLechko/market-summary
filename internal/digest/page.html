Terms

Basis point (bp)
One hundredth of a percentage point. A rate moving from 4.00% to 4.25% rose 25 bp.
Percentage point (pp)
The plain difference between two percentages. Inflation going from 3.0% to 3.2% is up 0.2 pp.
y/y
Compared with the same period a year earlier.
VIX
An index of how much the S&P 500 is expected to swing over the next month, read from options prices. Around 15 is calm; above 30 means investors are fearful.
Treasury yield
The interest rate the US government pays to borrow. Mortgages, car loans, and corporate debt are priced off it, so higher yields mean costlier borrowing everywhere.
10-year minus 2-year spread
When short-term rates sit above long-term rates (a negative spread), it has historically come before recessions.
High-yield credit spread
The extra interest riskier companies pay over Treasuries. When it widens, lenders are getting nervous.
CPI and core PCE
Two inflation measures. Core PCE leaves out food and energy, and is the one the Fed watches most closely; its target is 2%.
Payrolls
The number of jobs employers added that month.
Initial jobless claims
New applications for unemployment benefits each week. A rising trend is an early sign of layoffs.
Diluted EPS
Earnings per share: profit divided by the number of shares, counting shares that options and convertible securities could create.
Short interest
Shares that investors have borrowed and sold, betting the price will fall, and not yet bought back. A rising level means more investors are betting against the company.
Days to cover
Shares sold short divided by average daily trading: roughly how many days of normal trading it would take short sellers to buy their shares back. A high number can make a sharp rise more likely if they all rush to buy.
Guidance
A company's own forecast for its upcoming results. Raising or cutting it often moves the stock more than the results themselves.
FOMC
The Federal Open Market Committee, the Fed group that sets short-term interest rates. It meets eight times a year.
Beige Book
The Fed's report on business conditions in each of its twelve regions, built from conversations with local businesses.
